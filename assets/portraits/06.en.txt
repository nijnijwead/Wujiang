Dazhi is the boss's WeChat name.
Weizhen Noodle Shop was my second home in Wujiang.
After my first meal there, I was set on this place. Apart from KFC, every meal I ate in town was from their shop—by my rough count, twenty or thirty times.
Every time I walked in, seeing the two of them felt especially warm.

Many times, before going fishing with JA, we'd stop at the shop for noodles. The owner and his wife would always laugh when they saw us head out with empty buckets and come back with empty buckets.
The owner's wife even promised that if we caught anything, she'd stew it for us. Every time we'd say, "This time for sure," but we still haven't had that stewed fish.

The owner loved music, and his WeChat Moments were full of concert videos.
After he learned I was in a band, he asked me how the band was doing every time I came in.
Most of the songs our band covered were unfamiliar to him, but he really did like Dou Wei back in the day.
He told me about how much he'd loved Dou Wei when he was young, and he always wished for our band to keep going.
The owner quit smoking around January this year. He's gotten noticeably uglier since then, but he still asks about the band.
