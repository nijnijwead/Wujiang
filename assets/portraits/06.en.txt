This man was the only person who ever asked me to take his photo.
I understood that wandering the streets with a camera probably made me look even more like an outsider. This man was also from another city, and perhaps for that reason I never felt like an outsider around him.
He sold all kinds of hygiene products on the street, as if dragging around a treasure chest. An old woman asked if he had anything for getting rid of rats:
"Imported. Guaranteed to work."

He kept asking what pose he should strike. I was too embarrassed to tell him I wasn't some great photographer, and I hoped he didn't think my photos would end up on the news.
