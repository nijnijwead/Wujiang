Belial (A monster from Ultraman, 贝利亚) was a kid who played at the court, maybe in fifth or sixth grade.
When he guarded me, he liked to shout the names of various Ultraman characters and monsters. Every time he shouted "Belial," I missed my shot. Eventually he started calling me Belial, and I copied him and called him Belial too.
Belial was too young, so I hesitated about whether to give him a camera. Then I happened to meet his older brother.
His brother was in eleventh grade and studied in the county high school.
We met at the court. As the only two high schoolers on the "kids' court," we naturally ended up matched against each other. He was especially solid under the basket and hardly ever missed. After everyone left, we played one-on-one for a while longer. We talked as we played, and that was how I learned he was Belial's brother.

When he received the camera, he also hesitated, perhaps uncertainty about my motives.
Once I explained that it was just a small personal project, he stopped resisting.
Later he told me that Belial had grabbed the camera and taken some of the photos himself. The two of them finished quickly—in under a week.
