The Villagers

“The Villagers” photographs the animals in the town. They outnumber people and seem like the real owners. People have houses and storefronts; animals have cracks, rooftops, wall corners, roads at night, and moments when no one is watching. In a town like this, an animal often makes people linger longer than a storefront does, because it is evidence that the town is still living.

By day, the town belongs to people. At night, animals without a home come out, and the night belongs to them. The English title is “The Villagers” because they, too, are owners of the town—and they are often quicker to accept an outsider and play host.
