The Reflections

“The Reflections” photographs me—the photographer. I record a fact that is easily overlooked: every time the shutter is pressed, a body, a position, and a biased “I” are always present.

Mirrors are everywhere in the town. They keep sending the photographer back into the frame, reminding me that I am not recording Wujiang transparently; I am organizing Wujiang through my own existence, experience, and judgment. Existence is the development of subjectivity: the mirror is not a narcissistic prop, but a way to let the viewer see the viewer. This series makes the usually overlooked photographer part of the scene again, and turns “I see” into “I stand here, seeing through my own eyes.”
