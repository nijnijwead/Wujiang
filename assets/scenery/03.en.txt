The Forgotten

“The Forgotten” looks at things that have been forgotten, broken, or taken out of use. They may look like the most tragic subjects: once damaged, abandoned, or withdrawn from use, all that remains is to be looked at and remembered with nostalgia.

This series does not treat brokenness as an aesthetic, nor does it treat tragedy as their only identity. Brokenness is evidence left by use, repair, and time. The so-called “souls of the dead” are not ghosts of the dead, but things that remain in place while no longer being treated as part of life. Their dignity lies in having been used, relied on, and repaired. Though they may already be on the verge of being forgotten, they carry the marks of having once existed.
